# Lootbox Roulette - **Heavy Drinker Edition** 🥃💀

**Extreme Overwatch-themed drinking game for opening Loot Boxes**  
*For experienced heavy drinkers only. This version hits hard.*

---

### Setup
- One Loot Box = one full round (**4 items** per box).
- Best played with 2–6 people. Solo is possible but brutal.
- Use **Jaloviina** as the standard drink.
- Have multiple bottles ready — this game goes fast.
- Play while opening loot boxes live in Overwatch 2.

---

### Special Rules & Challenges
- **4 Commons in one box** → Everyone takes **3 Jaloviina**.
- **First Legendary** → “**God Tier Luck**” – Opener chooses **two people** to finish their drinks with them.
- **Epic Streak** (2+ Epics) → “**Purple Hell**” – Everyone takes **4 Jaloviina**.
- **All different categories** → “**Perfect Chaos**” – Opener is safe for the next box. Everyone else takes **2 Jaloviina**.
- **Pity Legendary** → Whole group does a **cheers** and everyone takes **3 Jaloviina**.
- **Weapon Charm / Souvenir** → “**Trash Win**” – **2 Jaloviina** + everyone roasts you.
- **3+ Legendaries in one session** → You become the **Jaloviina King** — you choose one rule for the rest of the night.

---

### Advanced / Chaotic Variants
- **Main Hero Curse**: Item for your main → **double** the Jaloviina. Item for a hero you hate → pass **half** your drinks to the person on your left.
- **Team Deathmatch Mode**: Everyone opens one box per round. Person with the **lowest total Jaloviina** this round takes **+5 penalty Jaloviina**.
- **No Dupe Run**: Zero duplicates → Everyone except the opener takes **4 Jaloviina**.
- **Sudden Death**: If someone pulls a Legendary skin for Genji, Hanzo, or Tracer → **Everyone finishes their current drink**.

### ⚠️ HEAVY DRINKER WARNING
- This game is **intentionally very strong**. Hydrate aggressively between rounds.
- Know your limits. Have a designated sober person or plan to crash safely.
- Never drink and drive. If anyone feels sick, stop immediately.
- Best played with people you trust.
