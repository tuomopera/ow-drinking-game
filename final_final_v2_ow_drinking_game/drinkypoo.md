### Lootbox Roulette 🍻

**Overwatch-themed drinking game for opening Loot Boxes**

### To prep
- Get your top 3 heroes for each ingame role for the **current season**

### Basic rules
- If loot is for your top 3 heroes (for each category), **drink double**

#### Core Rules (Per Item)

| Item / Rarity              | Action
|----------------------------|-----------------------------------------------------
| **Rare** (blue)            | 1 drink 
| **Epic** (purple)          | 2 drinks
| **Legendary** (gold)       | 3 drinks
| **Emote / Highlight Intro**| 2 drink 
| **Voice Line**             | 1 drink + repeat the voice line out loud
| **Victory poses**          | 1 drink
| **Loot box full of shit**  | No loot for anyones top 3 hero - 5 drinks for all because fuck this game

#### Special Rules & Challenges
- **Legendary for your own hero** --> the opener picks someone to drink with them (half of the openers amount)
- **If you equip the loot**       --> the opener drinks 1 extra
