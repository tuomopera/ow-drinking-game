### Lootbox Roulette 🍻

**Overwatch-themed drinking game for opening Loot Boxes**

#### Setup
- One Loot Box = one round (**4 items** per box).
- Play with friends or solo (use sips instead of shots when playing solo).
- Have your drink of choice ready (beer, cocktails, shots, etc.).
- Optional: Open the loot boxes in the menu while playing the game.

#### Core Rules (Per Item)

| Item / Rarity              | Action                                              | Drink Level    |
|----------------------------|-----------------------------------------------------|----------------|
| **Common**                 | Take a sip                                          | Small          |
| **Rare** (blue)            | Normal drink / gulp                                 | Medium         |
| **Epic** (purple)          | Take a shot or big chug                             | Heavy          |
| **Legendary** (gold)       | Finish your current drink + refill                  | Very Heavy     |
| **Duplicate**              | Extra sip for every dupe                            | Small          |
| **Skin**                   | Drink + say the hero’s name in a silly voice        | Medium         |
| **Emote / Highlight Intro**| Do the emote yourself + drink                       | Medium         |
| **Voice Line**             | Repeat the voice line out loud + drink              | Small          |
| **Spray / Icon / Charm**   | Quick sip                                           | Small          |

#### Special Rules & Challenges
- **4 Commons in one box** → Everyone drinks (or take 2 sips if solo).
- **First Legendary of the session** → “Legendary Luck” – the opener picks someone to drink with them.
- **Epic streak** (2+ Epics in one box) → “Purple Rain” – take a shot + everyone claps.
